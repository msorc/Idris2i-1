The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The dog, being lazy, did not jump;
the fox (quick as ever) jumped again and again over the dog's back.
"Quick!" said the fox. "Lazy?" said the dog. It's 5 o'clock: fox-time, dog-time,
and time for THE END. Brown fox, brown dog, brown fields, brown end.
An end is an end is an end.
